# pension-fund-carbon-dashboard
Power BI dashboard which estimates a pension fund's financed emissions and carbon price exposure.

**Context** : 
A pension fund is composed of a variety of investments including stocks, bonds and other assets. 

An important metric in investing is that of a financed emissions, which is the share of each company's greenhouse gas emissions that is attributed to the fund, based on how much of the company it owns. 

Funds track this because carbon prices and regulation can reduce the value of high-emitting company and in turn the value of the fund's investments. 


**Case study :** 
This project estimates the financed emissions of a hypothetical pension fund, identifies which holdings drive them as well as tests how different carbon prices would affect the portfolio.
